Create a sleek, futuristic intro animation for a desktop application called **“Vibe Control”**.

The core concept is:

**Many independent applications are being gathered and unified under one central control system.**

### Animation Sequence

1. **Opening Scene**

   * Start with a dark, minimal desktop-like environment.
   * Many different application icons are scattered randomly across the screen.
   * The icons should feel slightly chaotic and disconnected.
   * They can have subtle floating, drifting, or idle movements.
   * Avoid making the scene look playful or cartoonish. It should feel like a professional operating system or control interface.

2. **System Activation**

   * A subtle signal, pulse, light, or energy appears at the center of the screen.
   * The scattered application icons react to this central signal.
   * They begin moving smoothly toward the center.
   * Their movement should feel elegant and coordinated, as if a central system has taken control of them.
   * Use smooth easing, curved trajectories, slight depth, and staggered timing rather than having every icon move at exactly the same time.

3. **Convergence**

   * As the icons approach the center, their chaotic arrangement gradually becomes structured and organized.
   * Visually communicate that multiple independent applications are now connected to and controlled by a single central system.
   * The icons may orbit briefly, form a network, collapse into a central symbol, or merge into one abstract control-system core.
   * Use subtle connecting lines, particles, light trails, or digital signals if helpful.
   * Keep the effect clean and sophisticated rather than overly flashy.

4. **Final Transformation**

   * The gathered icons fully converge into one unified central system.
   * The central system should feel stable, powerful, intelligent, and organized.
   * After the convergence completes, transition smoothly into the project title:

   **VIBE CONTROL**

   * The title should appear cleanly and confidently in the center.
   * Optional subtitle:
     **Unified Control System**

5. **Ending**

   * Hold on the “VIBE CONTROL” logo/title for a short moment.
   * Add a subtle glow, pulse, or finishing animation.
   * End cleanly so the animation can transition naturally into the application's main UI.

### Visual Style

* Futuristic desktop operating system
* Premium software product intro
* Minimal and sophisticated
* Dark background
* Subtle glassmorphism / holographic UI elements
* Smooth motion
* Elegant motion graphics
* Soft glow and light trails
* Slight 3D depth
* High-tech but not cyberpunk
* Clean rather than visually noisy
* Similar feeling to a modern AI operating system, command center, or unified desktop controller

### Motion Direction

The visual story should clearly communicate:

**Chaos → Central signal → Attraction → Organization → Unification → Vibe Control**

The most important part is that the viewer instantly understands that **Vibe Control is the central system that brings multiple applications together and controls them from one place.**

Target duration: approximately **5–8 seconds**.

Avoid:

* excessive explosions
* aggressive glitch effects
* gaming-style visuals
* excessive neon colors
* cartoon-like icon movement
* icons simply disappearing without visually showing convergence
* overly complicated backgrounds
