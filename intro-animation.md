Create a short intro animation for a project called "vibe control".

Concept:
"vibe control" is a central system that unifies and controls multiple applications across a computer environment. The animation should visually communicate the idea of scattered tools and apps being brought together into one organized, intelligent control system.

Style direction:
Use the emotional and motion-language inspiration of a hardware sampler / sequencer similar to the KO2 vibe — not as a literal music device, but as a rhythmic, tactile, grid-based control experience.
The result should feel clean, modern, functional, and slightly playful, with rhythmic motion and strong control-system energy.

Important:
Do NOT make it look like a music production app or DJ interface.
Do NOT focus on audio waveforms, turntables, musical notes, or obvious beat-making visuals.
Instead, translate the KO2 feeling into:
- rhythmic activation
- tactile pad-like responses
- step-sequencer style timing
- minimal hardware-inspired UI motion
- pulse-based feedback
- structured, satisfying organization

Storyboard (revised 2026-09-09 — organized grid, NOT scattered):
Layout principle: the app "pads" are NOT randomly scattered. They are laid out
as a calm, organized grid of five horizontal rows, centered on screen, and the
project logo sits in the exact center as the clear focal point. The pads keep
their tactile pad/glyph design but are held faded (low opacity) so they read as
an atmospheric control surface and never compete with or out-emphasize the logo.

1. Opening:
A centered five-row grid of tool pads is present from the start — orderly and
structured, not chaotic. The pads are dim/faded, quietly waiting.

2. Activation:
A subtle activation begins at the exact center and ripples outward.
The pads respond ring-by-ring with rhythmic, step-sequencer timing — each
snapping "on" with a soft tactile pad-press flash — but they stay faded overall.

3. Center focus:
As the ripple settles, the logo resolves in the center — the orange record-dot
mark snapping in with a slight overshoot, then the "vibe control" wordmark.
A soft radial spotlight lifts the logo off the faded grid so it is unmistakably
the focal point; the pads are clearly subordinate.

4. Final reveal:
The composition settles: an organized, faded grid of tools arranged around one
central, sharply-lit logo — many tools, one control system.
End with a subtle final confirmation pulse from the logo mark.

Visual tone:
- sleek and minimal
- modern UI / system aesthetic
- rhythmic and satisfying motion
- grid/pad-inspired animation language
- controlled chaos becoming order
- premium but not flashy
- calm confidence, not aggressive energy

Motion keywords:
smooth, magnetic, synchronized, pulsing, tactile, modular, precise, responsive, elegant, organized

Color / atmosphere:
Keep it refined and tech-forward.
A dark or neutral background is okay, with controlled accent lighting.
Use subtle highlights and interface glow, but avoid excessive neon or cyberpunk overload.

Goal:
The viewer should immediately understand:
"Many scattered tools are being brought together into one central control system."
It should feel like vibe control turns chaos into rhythm, and fragmentation into unified control.
